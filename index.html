Herobrine Add-On

Summon the legend into your Minecraft Bedrock world. Includes custom Behavior and Resource packs.

Download .mcaddon (Free)
How to Install:
  1. Download herobrine.mcaddon above.
  2. Double-click the file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock.
  3. Activate both the Behavior and Resource packs in your world settings.
  4. Run /summon custom:herobrine in-game!
